# AI & agents

![AI and agents: a copper network of connected reasoning nodes](../../assets/categories/ai-agents.svg)

I study how models investigate, critique, and revise answers, and build systems that preserve the evidence behind their actions.

## Start here

| Project | What to explore |
| --- | --- |
| [Mini-AURION](https://github.com/billyrdavis1985-bot/Mini_AURION) | A governed multi-model research testbed with provider routing, spend receipts, and operator controls. Start with its `VISION.md` and the committed demos. |
| [Forge Agent](https://github.com/billyrdavis1985-bot/forge-agent) | Sandboxed evaluations of local reasoning critics, with provenance tracking and blinded adjudication. |
| [HF Critic](https://github.com/billyrdavis1985-bot/hf-critic) | Fine-tuning Qwen and Mistral into reasoning-process critics, with held-out evaluation and a metric audit. |

## Follow the benchmark series

| Study | Research question |
| --- | --- |
| [HF-IQR V1](https://github.com/billyrdavis1985-bot/-IRMB_HF-IQR_ReasoningBenchmark) | How do frontier models perform in a structured reasoning-process evaluation? |
| [HF-IQR V2](https://github.com/billyrdavis1985-bot/HF-IQR-V2-Hudson-Forge-Intelligence-and-Reasoning-Benchmark) | How do critique behavior, position stability, and revisions change across deliberation rounds? |
| [HF-IQR V3](https://github.com/billyrdavis1985-bot/HF-IQR-V3) | What changes when models are required to produce counterarguments? |
| [MTCA research](https://github.com/billyrdavis1985-bot/mtca-research) | How sensitive is frontier-model reasoning to the way non-propositional content is framed? |

The repository links are the sources for these summaries. A testbed, benchmark, or working demo has its own scope; consult each project's evaluation and limitations before interpreting a result.
